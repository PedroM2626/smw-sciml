### 10.70 What the Way Across `0x27` Requires, and Clearing the Fourth Stage by Frontier Archive Search

10.69 cleared `0x29` under the potential-maximum reward and stopped on `0x27` at `x = 544.0625 px` (`528.0625 px` of progress, dying in the pit at frame 400). It left two questions explicitly unmeasured: what the way across that gap requires in the level's own tile buffer, and whether restarting from savestates along the progression frontier clears the stage under the same reward and the same 10.66 verdict. `src/training/frontier_archive_search.py` measures both.

**Why every greedy run from `t = 0` hits a wall at `x = 544.0625 px`.** Reading the 16-bit Map16 buffer of `smw_map_round_1_152_104_t27.state` across the first screen (`cols 0..40`, `x = 0..640 px`) shows a deceptive lower floor:

1. **The lower-floor trap (`row 24, y = 384 px`).** Mario starts at `(x = 16.0, y = 352.125)` on the ground floor (`row 24`, tiles `0x0145`, `0x0100`, `0x0149`), which is solid only up to column 18 (`x = 304 px`). Columns 22..32 (`x = 352..512 px`) at row 24 hold tile `0x006B` (dotted outline scenery, measured in 10.68 as non-solid decor over air), and column 34 (`x = 544 px`) is the vertical wall of a pillar whose top platform sits at `row 16 (y = 256 px)` — 8 rows (`128 px`) above the entry floor.
2. **Physical unreachability from the lower ledge.** A full-speed sprint jump launched from the edge of column 18 (`x = 283.1, y = 352.1, vx = 48.0, vy = -83.0`) reaches its apex at `(x = 366.8, y = 263.1)` — already below the `y = 256 px` top of the pillar — and strikes the pillar's left wall at `x = 544.0625 px` (`y = 456.1 px`) with `vx = 0.0` and `vy = 70.0` before falling past the buffer into mode `0x0B` at frame 400. A swinging brown platform (`sprite 0x5F`) spawns in slot 7 at `(472, 264)`, and a single solid block (`0x0130`) sits at `col 24, row 16 (x = 384, y = 256 px)`.
3. **The stepped upper route (`row 20 -> row 16 -> row 12 -> row 9`).** Directly above the starting floor sit four stepped grass platforms (`0x0100`/`0x0101`/`0x0104`): `row 20 (y = 320 px)` at columns `[6, 7, 8, 9, 10]`, `row 16 (y = 256 px)` at columns `[3, 4, 5, 6, 7]`, `row 12 (y = 192 px)` at columns `[5, 6, 7, 8, 9]`, and `row 9 (y = 144 px)` at columns `[8, 9, 10, 11, 12]`. Because the 10.69 observation window spans `WINDOW_ROWS = range(-3, 4)` (`48 px` above Mario) and single-shot episodes from `t = 0` earn immediate positive reward by running right along `row 24` (`x = 16 -> 283 px`), a population selected by total episode return from `t = 0` locks into the lower-floor local optimum in generation 0 and never climbs the `240 px` vertical staircase at `x = 48..208 px`.

**Cell-archive frontier search (Go-Explore).** Instead of mutating a policy from `t = 0` on every rollout, `src/training/frontier_archive_search.py` maintains a deterministic archive of visited kinematic-spatial cells keyed by `(int(x // 20), int(y // 20), vy_tier, vx_tier)`, where `vy_tier` separates grounded/apex (`1`), ascending (`2`, `vy < -10`), and falling (`0`) states, and `vx_tier` separates sprint speed (`2`, `vx >= 38`), normal rightward motion (`1`), and leftward motion (`-1`). Each cell stores the emulator savestate and action sequence that achieved the highest within-cell score. On each iteration the search expands up to 10 unvisited cells along the horizontal frontier and up to 6 along the elevation frontier across 28 deterministic macro-primitives (the 8 button combinations of 10.69 held for 1, 2, or 3 decisions of 4 frames, plus 4 sprint-jump macros).

**`0x27` is cleared — all four stages the map hands over are now concluded on the console's own terms.** Climbing the initial staircase reaches `y = 91.7 px` above the first gap and crosses `x = 544 px` at iteration 52 (`x = 569.9 px`), lands on the upper platform at `x = 613.6 px, y = 224.7 px`, and progresses across the stage's middle to a second vertical barrier at column 235 (`x = 3762.9 px`, where a wall rises to `row 10, y = 160 px`). Because the archive keys `vx_tier = 2` (`vx >= 38`) separately from walking arrivals, it preserves the sprint run-up needed to clear that wall at iteration 96 (`x = 4136.4 px`) and touches the goal tape at `x = 5081.6250 px` (`progress = 5065.6250 px`, simple-reward return `326.6016` including the `+10` clear bonus). The end-level timer at `$7E:1493` fires on frame `2251`; releasing the controls at frame `2252` for `600` frames lets the engine execute its exit march `0x14 -> 0x0c -> 0x0d -> 0x0e` with the lives counter at `$7E:0DBE` untouched (`4 -> 4`). Replayed from `smw_map_round_1_152_104_t27.state` in a fresh emulator process both with and without the 10.60 `PlayerHold` message-box operator, the saved 563-step sequence reproduces identically (`5065.6250 px`, `2252` frames, `cleared = True`, `died = False`, lives `4 -> 4`). Like 10.69's genome on `0x29`, this trajectory is a deterministic open-loop witness from the stage's savestate that settles the solvability and exploration hypothesis of 10.68–10.69, not a closed-loop generalisation rate.
